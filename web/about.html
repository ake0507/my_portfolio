About Me

Creative, collaborative, and detail-focused — that's how I approach every project.

Hi, I'm Aklilu Desalegn

I've been crafting eye-catching graphics for two years, helping businesses tell their story through design. My specialties include engaging social media graphics, memorable logos and branding, attention-grabbing YouTube thumbnails, and striking print designs like flyers and business cards.

I love collaborating with clients to bring their vision to life and make their brand stand out. My design approach is user-centric and detail-oriented, striving to create visuals that elevate a brand's identity.

Whether you need a complete brand identity or a single social media post, I bring the same level of care and creativity to every project.

My Design Approach

Every project follows a thoughtful process to deliver results that matter.

1. Discover

I start by understanding your brand, audience, and goals. Research and conversation help me grasp what makes your project unique.

2. Design

With a clear direction, I create concepts that align with your vision. I iterate based on your feedback until the design feels right.

3. Deliver

You receive polished, production-ready files along with any brand guidelines needed to keep your visuals consistent going forward.
